About Us

Built By Fundraisers, For Fundraisers

FundRise was founded in 2014 by a group of former nonprofit development directors who saw a gap: brilliant organizations doing life-changing work, held back by the complexity of grant writing and fundraising strategy.

Today, we partner with over 600 nonprofits from grassroots community groups to national institutions? helping them articulate their impact, find aligned funders, and build revenue engines that sustain their missions for the long haul.

We're not just writers. We're strategists, researchers, and storytellers who believe every mission deserves to be funded.

Our Mission

Why We Do What We Do

To democratize access to funding for nonprofits of every size � so that the best ideas, not the biggest budgets, win.

Equity in Funding

We believe small and mid-sized nonprofits deserve the same access to grants as large institutions. Our tiered pricing and capacity-building programs make that possible.

Strategy Over Templates

No copy-paste proposals. Every grant we write is rooted in research, tailored to the funder, and built on your unique story and data.

Long-Term Partnership

We don't disappear after submission. From award management to renewal strategy, we stay with you to build sustainable funding streams.

2014

FundRise Founded

Three former development directors launch FundRise from a shared office, serving 8 local nonprofits in their first year.

2017

$50M Milestone

2020

Nonprofit Relief Initiative

2023
