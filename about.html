About Us

Built By Fundraisers, For Fundraisers

FundRise was founded in 2014 by a group of former nonprofit development directors who saw a gap: brilliant organizations doing life-changing work, held back by the complexity of grant writing and fundraising strategy.

Today, we partner with over 600 nonprofits from grassroots community groups to national institutions? helping them articulate their impact, find aligned funders, and build revenue engines that sustain their missions for the long haul.

We're not just writers. We're strategists, researchers, and storytellers who believe every mission deserves to be funded.

Our Mission & Purpose

Why We Do What We Do

To democratize access to institutional funding for nonprofits of every size — so that the most impactful community missions, not just the largest budgets, win.

Accessibility

Equity in Funding

Small and mid-sized nonprofits deliver essential front-line impact but are routinely out-spent on grant infrastructure. We level the playing field through accessible embedded teams and capacity-building programs.

600+ Grassroots & Mid-Sized Groups Served
Authenticity

Strategy Over Templates

Grant reviewers discard boilerplates in minutes. Every application we architect is built from local community data, customized logic models, and rigorous programmatic alignment with each funder's stated priorities.

100% Bespoke Logic Models & Narratives
Longevity

Long-Term Partnership

One-off grant submissions create unstable financial spikes. We build permanent pipeline architecture, post-award compliance systems, and funder renewal protocols that sustain our partners for years to come.

Our Differentiators

What Sets FundRise Apart

We are not an automated software portal or an unverified freelance marketplace. We are seasoned development practitioners committed to measurable results.

Precision

Research-Driven Matching

We avoid spray-and-pray applications. Our proprietary 990 tax intelligence analyzes giving history, median grant awards, and trustee connections to pinpoint high-probability funders.

Practitioners

Writers Who've Evaluated Grants

Our consultants have evaluated federal proposals and directed in-house nonprofit development teams. We understand institutional scoring rubrics from the inside out.

Transparency

Live Pipeline Tracking

Every client receives direct portal access to track submission progress, active reviewer feedback, grant calendar deadlines, and secured dollars in real-time.

Predictable

Predictable Fixed Pricing

Empowerment
