Campaign Planning

Whether it's an annual fund, a capital campaign, or a giving day � we plan the goal, the timeline, the volunteer structure, and the ask strategy end-to-end.

We've run campaigns from $50K to $15M. Each one starts with a feasibility check, a case for support, and a quiet-phase roadmap before public launch.

Phase 1 � Feasibility & Case for Support

6�8 weeks of donor interviews and goal-setting

Phase 2 � Quiet Phase (60�70% of goal)

Lead gift solicitations with board and insiders

Phase 3 � Public Launch
