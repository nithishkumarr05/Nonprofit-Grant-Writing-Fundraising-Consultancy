Practical Track

Fundraising Tips

Short, actionable tips you can apply this week. No fluff, no filler — just what works in the field.

Donor Retention

Send a thank-you within 48 hours

Donors who receive a personal thank-you within two days are 4x more likely to give again. Build a 48-hour thank-you SOP for your team.

Email Appeals

Write the subject line last

Your subject line should summarize the single most compelling idea in the email. Draft the body first, then pull the punchiest phrase up top.

Prospect Research

Look for 3 signals, not 1

A single signal (wealth, affinity, or prior giving) is weak. Three together means it's time to reach out. Score every prospect on all three.

Grants

Track win/loss reasons, not just results

After every declined proposal, ask the funder (politely) why. Over 12 months, the patterns will tell you exactly where to focus.

Major Gifts

Ask for a conversation, not a gift

"Could I share what we're working on?" out-performs "Can you give $X?" every time. The gift comes from the conversation.

Board Engagement

Give every board member 3 names

Boards freeze at "help fundraise." Instead, assign each member 3 specific prospects to introduce. Clarity drives action.
