Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with FundRise.

What types of nonprofits do you work with?

We partner with 501(c)(3) organizations across healthcare, education, community housing, environmental justice, youth development, and human services. Our clients range from emerging organizations with $500K annual budgets to established networks with $25M+ budgets.

How quickly can we submit our first grant?

For single grant sprints with imminent deadlines, our rapid response team can construct, audit, and submit a proposal in as little as 10 to 14 business days. For full pipeline retainers, we conduct discovery in Weeks 1–2 and begin submissions in Month 1.

Do you work on a commission or contingency fee basis?

No. Working on a percentage of funds raised violates the ethical guidelines of the Association of Fundraising Professionals (AFP) and can disqualify nonprofits from federal funding. We operate exclusively on transparent, predictable fixed fees agreed upon up front.

Can you augment our existing in-house development team?

Yes. Many of our clients have in-house development staff who focus on major donors and events while our team handles the technical research, drafting, and compliance of large foundation and federal submissions.
